# nyatodo

A personal, keyboard-first task and time-tracking workspace designed for low-friction capture, thought structuring, and distraction-free focus.

## Language

**Task**:
A single unit of work tracked in the unified **Task List**, carrying a **Task Status**, optional **Requester**, **Thinking Space**, and **Work Sessions**.
_Avoid_: Ticket, issue, inbox item, card

**Task List**:
The single primary collection holding every **Task** without a separate triage bucket or inbox.
_Avoid_: Backlog, inbox, board

**Requester**:
The person or source (for example, a colleague from Teams) who asked for a **Task** to be done.
_Avoid_: Assignee, reporter, attribution, link

**Task Status**:
The state of a **Task**: `Todo` (ready to work on), `Doing` (actively in progress), `Waiting` (paused on someone else before proceeding), or `Done` (completed).
_Avoid_: Stage, column, priority

**Thinking Space**:
An expandable area inside a **Task** holding freeform notes and **Sub-steps** for structuring thoughts and preserving context.
_Avoid_: Description, wiki, sub-page

**Sub-step**:
A concrete, checkable action item nested inside a **Task**'s **Thinking Space**.
_Avoid_: Sub-task, child ticket, checklist card

**Task Timer**:
A toggleable per-**Task** stopwatch that runs independently or alongside other **Task Timers**.
_Avoid_: Focus lock, Pomodoro clock

**Work Session**:
A recorded duration of work—captured live by a **Task Timer** or entered manually—belonging to a **Task**.
_Avoid_: Timesheet, billable entry, Pomodoro

**Daily Summary**:
A local chronological view of **Work Sessions** and completed **Tasks** grouped by calendar day for personal review.
_Avoid_: Standup report, export, timesheet
